FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this just another dating app?

Not really. Most apps optimize for how long you stay in the app. IRL optimizes for how quickly you get to a real date — with a weekly rhythm and reviewed profiles instead of endless swiping.

How is this different from Tinder or Bumble?

Those apps give you unlimited matches and leave the rest to you. IRL gives you a small number of curated, reviewed introductions each week and structures the path from "matched" to "met in person" — including a private way to signal interest after the date.

Where does IRL launch first?

Ho Chi Minh City. We're building the founding cohort here first, starting with people already active in the city's professional and sports communities.

Do I need to pay?

Joining the waitlist is free, and founding members get free early access. We'll always be upfront before anything ever requires payment.

What if I don't click with my introductions?

That's what Thursday's Second Chance round is for — a fresh set of introductions before the week is over, so one quiet week doesn't mean an empty weekend.
