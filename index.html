Why IRL

What makes IRL different

Three decisions that change what dating feels like — before, during, and after the date. Meeting sooner shouldn't mean trusting blindly.

✓

Reviewed Profiles

Reviewed, not rated. We check that profiles are real, complete, and give someone enough context to feel comfortable meeting — so trust is built upfront, not earned after a week of back-and-forth texting.

◆

Fewer, Better Introductions

We trade infinite swiping for a handful of intentional introductions a week — quality curation over endless scroll.

How it works

Your week with IRL

One rhythm, every week — designed to end in a real date, not a longer chat thread.

Monday – Thursday

Set your intention, meet your introductions

Tell IRL what you're looking for, then meet a handful of reviewed profiles — chosen with intention, not swiped through by the hundred. If you're both curious, a short window opens to get comfortable, and if nothing clicks by Thursday, a fresh Second Chance round goes out before the week is over.

Friday

Commit to a real plan

Lock in a time. Figure out the place together. No more "we should hang out sometime."

Weekend

Meet in person

The whole point. An actual date, at an actual place, in your actual city.

After

Answer "See Again?"

A private, low-pressure signal after the date — less guessing, a clearer sense of where things stand. If you both say yes, IRL helps you make the next plan.

Friday

Chat has one job: help you actually show up.

IRL doesn't put your whole date inside a form. You talk normally — the product only structures the one thing that matters most: when you're actually meeting.

Mutual curiosity means "you seem worth getting to know." A confirmed time means "we're ready to meet."
